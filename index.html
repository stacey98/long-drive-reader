Prepare whole reading

Voices marked Premium, Enhanced or Natural sound the most human. On iPhone with iOS 26, download them in Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices → English (on older iOS: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English). Ava, Zoe and Evan are good picks. Then close Safari fully and reopen this page. To be honest: Safari may still offer web pages only the phone’s built-in voices, not the ones you download. The check below shows exactly what it offers.

    This reading looks like a play. Each character gets their own voice when this device has enough good (Premium, Enhanced or Natural) voices; otherwise they share yours and their names are read out. Tap a character to try another good voice.

    Use it like an app

    In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens straight to your last document, at the sentence where you stopped. For a proper icon instead of a blank one: save the Long Drive Reader icon to Photos, then in the Shortcuts app make a shortcut with the action "Open URLs" and this page's link, choose Add to Home Screen, and pick the icon from Photos.

    On the website copy of this app (not inside claude.ai), the app keeps a copy of itself on the phone after a visit online, so it can open with no signal. Your Library readings are on the phone anyway.
